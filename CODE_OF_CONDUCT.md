# Code of conduct

Wick welcomes respectful participation from people of every background and
experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and considerate.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy, consent, and the repository's clean-room rule.
- Use examples with placeholder personal data and redact credentials.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, security disclosure, and design
  fidelity even when you disagree.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, publishing
private vulnerability details before a coordinated fix, or deliberately
circumventing project safety and licensing rules are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainer or, when a
security issue is involved, through the process in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers
may edit or remove contributions, lock discussions, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled as privately as the
circumstances allow.

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, project-operated
community spaces, and public representation of the project.
